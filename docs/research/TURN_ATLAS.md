# Turn atlas

Every swing point of the exploration slice (three-candle and engulfing), scored by what it delivered and read against the context it formed in. Descriptive: no verdict, no ledger row. A value that stands out is a lead to pre-register, not a finding (algo-research update 2026-10g).

- **held**: price traded beyond the objective before trading beyond the swing's extreme; a touch is neither. `crt` is the candle range's other side, `same_tf` the origin of the leg into the swing, `next_tf` the next timeframe's untaken swing.
- **geometry**: the chance that a driftless random walk from the confirming close reaches the objective first, (close - extreme) / (objective - extreme). **Excess** = held rate - geometry: what the swing delivered beyond where its confirming candle had already put price (AR-D33).
- **vs the rest**: a value's excess minus the excess of the three-candle swings without it.
- **qualifies**: at least 100 swings on 40 trading dates. Differences are in percentage points with 95% intervals from resampled trading dates.
